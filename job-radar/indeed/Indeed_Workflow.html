Indeed workflow

Reads the newest Indeed India jobs for the roles in input.csv: several searches in parallel, each with its own random pauses, backing off if Indeed pushes back. Indeed also sends the full description, salary, job type and company details, so this workflow pulls out as much as it can: experience, qualification, skills, contacts, salary per month and more, and applies the role's minimum experience. Click any node for the technical detail.

1. The input you give it

The same shared file as every other site: job-radar-V3/input.csv.

Role,Keywords,Experience,Location
AI Engineer,"AI Engineer;LLM Engineer",5,India
Clinical Research,"Pharmacovigilance;Medical Writer",0,India
  • Role: label for the group; becomes the Role column.
  • Keywords: titles separated by semicolons; each is its own search.
  • Experience: minimum years. Indeed's text is searched for the experience a job asks for; jobs whose range is below the minimum are dropped, jobs that state none are kept.
  • Location: India, or a city (Bengaluru becomes "Bengaluru, India").
  • Command line: --hours N (default: automatic, see the time window note; 0 = no time filter, first backlog run only), --role "AI Engineer", --workers 3.

4. Why it is gentler than LinkedIn

  • One request chain per search returns everything for the window, so there is no ten-at-a-time paging.
  • Asking for 50 or 150 jobs returned the same 13 for "Data Scientist" in 24 hours: it is a small, fresh feed.
  • Each search still draws its own pause profile (3 to 7 s low, plus 4 to 9 s) and the first workers start staggered.
  • If Indeed pushes back (HTTP 403 or 429): wait 60 to 120 s and retry; give up on that search after two; stop the run after three searches gave up. Partial results are always saved.
  • No login, no stealth, no proxy.

5. What a real run gave (2026-10-11)

  • 15 roles, 44 searches, jobs posted within 24 hours, 3 workers: 94 unique jobs in 2.2 minutes (51 posted yesterday, 43 today). 196 jobs were fetched; the rest were repeats across keywords or dropped by the experience rule (15 jobs below the role's minimum). No pushback at all.
  • 29 of the 44 searches found nothing: Indeed carries almost no Ayurveda, drug-inspector or pharma-regulatory jobs. Those need the official sites.
  • By role: ML Engineer 31, AI Engineer 14, Data Scientist 13, Architect 13, Data Analyst 10, Drug and Regulatory 5, Clinical Research 4, Business Analyst 3, Ayurveda Pharma 1.
  • 60 of the 94 state an experience requirement, 36 show a salary, 11 are remote.
  • Details run: cap for runs without a time filter raised to 1,000 per search (largest search found 734). Non-tech roles, no time filter: 2,993 unique jobs in 3.0 minutes. Workbook now 3,105 jobs. Fill rates: description 98%, company page 92%, skills 81%, qualification 71%, experience 69%, apply link 58%, rating 54%, company website 45%, company info 44%, company size 42%, salary 40%, emails 11%, industry 8%, phones 6%.
  • First run without a time filter (--hours 0) for the 7 non-tech roles: 2,208 unique jobs in 1.9 minutes, 1,850 of them older than 7 days. The 24-hour window had hidden them: "Ayurvedic Doctor" gives 83 jobs and "BAMS Doctor" 201 without it. Use --hours 0 for a first backlog run only; the daily automation uses 24 hours.

6. Not in this workflow (done later, in the combined pipeline)

  • Title filtering and role routing: Indeed's keyword match is loose.
  • India and Bengaluru ranking, work-mode flags from the description, and removing duplicates across Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed and the others.
  • The full description is not stored here (a 300-character snippet is); the combined pipeline can keep it if needed.
  • The daily 9 AM schedule.

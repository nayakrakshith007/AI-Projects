Naukri workflow

Reads the newest Naukri jobs for the roles in input.csv with a real Chrome, then adds them to an Excel file. Click any node for the technical detail: what it does, which file and function, the settings, and what happens when it fails.

1. The input you give it

Edit job-radar-V3/input.csv (one file shared by every site), then run one command.

Role,Keywords,Experience,Location
AI Engineer,"AI Engineer;LLM Engineer",5,India
Clinical Research,"Pharmacovigilance;Medical Writer",0,India
  • Role: a label for the group; becomes the Role column in Excel.
  • Keywords: job titles separated by semicolons. Each is searched on its own.
  • Experience: minimum years, a number. A job stays if its range reaches it (3-6 years passes for 5) or states none. 0 or blank means no filter.
  • Location: one or more places separated by semicolons (India, Bengaluru ...).
  • How recent: by default automatic, from midnight of the latest posted date already read (at least 24 h, at most 30 days; a missed day is caught up). Override with --hours N (Naukri rounds up to its 1, 3, 7, 15, 30 day filter). Pick roles with --role "AI Engineer".

Columns: Title, Company, Role, Experience, Location, Skills, Snippet, Posted, Posted date, Search term, First seen, Link, Status (dropdown), Notes.

3. Commands (from job-radar-V3)

.venv\Scripts\python -m naukri search
.venv\Scripts\python -m naukri search --role "AI Engineer" --hours 48
.venv\Scripts\python -m naukri check      (or double-click check.bat)
.venv\Scripts\python -m pytest naukri/tests

Close Naukri.xlsx in Excel before a run. If it is open, a timestamped copy is written instead.

4. Safety rules built in

  • No login, no account, no credentials anywhere in the code.
  • The human check is never solved or hidden: the run stops, keeps what it has, and shows a pop-up.
  • No stealth patches, no spoofed fingerprint, no proxies. It is a normal Chrome with normal pauses.
  • Slow on purpose: 9 to 20 s between pages, 25 to 55 s between terms.
  • Everything personal (output/, the Chrome profile) is git-ignored.

5. What a real run gave (2026-10-11)

  • 9 search terms (before the CSV input existed), jobs posted within 24 hours. Later CSV smoke test: Forward Deployed Engineer gave 4 jobs, 3 after the 5+ years rule.
  • 597 unique jobs: 293 posted today, 302 yesterday, 2 unknown.
  • First term to find a job gets it: AI Engineer 260, ML Engineer 227, Data Scientist 60, Generative AI Engineer 21, LLM Engineer 21, Machine Learning Engineer 8.
  • No human check appeared. 5 automated tests pass.

6. Not in this workflow (done later, in the combined pipeline)

  • Full job descriptions (each would need one more page load per job).
  • Routing jobs to role tabs, the 5+ years filter, the India and Bengaluru ranking.
  • Removing duplicates across Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed and the others.
  • The daily 9 AM schedule.

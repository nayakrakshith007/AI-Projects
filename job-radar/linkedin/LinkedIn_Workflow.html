LinkedIn workflow

Reads the newest public LinkedIn jobs for the roles in input.csv: ten jobs per request, several searches in parallel, each with its own random pauses, backing off when LinkedIn pushes back. Then it adds the jobs to an Excel file. Click any node for the technical detail: what it does, the file and function, the settings, and what happens when it fails.

1. The input you give it

The same file as every other site: job-radar-V3/input.csv.

Role,Keywords,Experience,Location
AI Engineer,"AI Engineer;LLM Engineer",5,India
Clinical Research,"Pharmacovigilance;Medical Writer",0,India
  • Role: label for the group; becomes the Role column.
  • Keywords: titles separated by semicolons; each is its own search.
  • Experience: not applied on LinkedIn, because its job cards carry no experience text. Unstated jobs are always kept; the combined pipeline will infer experience later.
  • Location: India, or a city (Bengaluru becomes "Bengaluru, India").
  • Command line: --hours N (default: automatic, from the latest posted date already read), --role "AI Engineer", --workers 3, --engine jobspy|browser.

4. Why parallel, and why it is still polite

  • One search asking for 100 jobs at once got HTTP 429 after the first ten. Ten per request with a random pause did not.
  • Three searches at once cut the time by about a third. More workers raise the chance of a 429, so 3 is the default.
  • Every search draws its own pause range (low 8 to 15 s, plus 10 to 20 s of spread), so the workers do not tick in step. Starts are staggered.
  • On a 429: wait 60 to 120 s and retry the same page; give up on that search after two; stop the run after three searches gave up. Partial results are always saved.
  • No login, no stealth, no proxy, no check solving.

5. What a real run gave

  • Full run on 2026-10-11: 15 roles, 44 searches, jobs posted within 24 hours, 3 parallel workers. 1,096 unique jobs in 30.9 minutes (555 posted today, 541 yesterday), about 250 requests, 33 rate-limit hits (HTTP 429) that were backed off and retried, no search gave up.
  • Jobs per role (first search to find a job keeps it): ML Engineer 167, Architect 125, Business Analyst 115, Forward Deployed Engineer 107, Ayurveda Pharma 106, Ayurveda Consultant 87, Clinical Research 87, Data Analyst 79, Data Scientist 68, Drug Inspector 67, Ayurveda Faculty 22, Drug and Regulatory 22, Ayurveda Research 21, AI Engineer 18, AI Scientist 5. Many AI jobs sit under ML Engineer because that search ran first; the combined pipeline re-routes by title.

6. Not in this workflow (done later, in the combined pipeline)

  • Job descriptions and experience (LinkedIn needs one request per job; descriptions would also let the pipeline infer experience).
  • Title filtering and role routing: LinkedIn's keyword match is loose, so many results are not the role.
  • India and Bengaluru ranking, work-mode flags, and removing duplicates across Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed and the others.
  • The daily 9 AM schedule.
